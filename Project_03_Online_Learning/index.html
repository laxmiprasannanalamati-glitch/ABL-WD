Principles of Effective Interface Design

Clarity: Use simple headings and meaningful labels.

Consistency: Keep colors, fonts and spacing similar across sections.

Color theory: Choose colors that are comfortable to read.

Typography: Use readable font families and suitable font sizes.

Information architecture: Arrange related information under clear headings.

Mobile-first approach: Plan content so that it remains clear on small screens. The viewport setting helps the page use the device width.

Accessibility: Provide alternative text for meaningful images and descriptive links.
